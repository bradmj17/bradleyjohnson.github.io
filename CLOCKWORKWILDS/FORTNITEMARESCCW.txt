Clockwork Wilds is a round-based, zombie survival shooter featuring a diablo-like isometric arcade camera with up to four-player co-op. It takes place on a jungle island inhabited by ancient ruins, decorated by large rusted gears. As a team, players will earn points upon eliminating zombie combatants and be able to purchase weapons, ammunition, open paths, and more.

Some additional mechanics include:
-Interactive HUD: The game tracks which parts are collected and they will appear on all players' HUD. This is important to indicate to the player how they are progressing in the map's story, which parts they have, and which parts they may be missing.
-Crafting: There are two crafting tables throughout the map that players can collect parts (3 per crafted weapon) to create Kinetic Blade or a Riot Shield. These parts are scattered throughout in designated locations for players to discover.
-Upgrading: After the player aligns the West's Red Gear and the East's Blue Gear, the systems connect in the map's center where there is a large machine. Interacting with this machine, at the cost of 5000 weapons, will exchange the player's weapon for a more powerful version.
-Perks: The game features two perks. The red perk grants increased weapon damage, and the blue increases passive shield gain.
-Misc.: 
>There are about 60 total weapons in the map. 
>Players can spend points to ride a raft down the river (to be used in emergencies to escape a hoard of zombies)
>Unlimited Rounds (no cap; zombies required to complete a round starts at about 7 and increases reach round, capping out at 200 zombies per round.)
>Isometric Camera
